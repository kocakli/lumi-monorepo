## Description

Lumi is a small place on the internet where strangers write each other anonymous letters of kindness.

You write a single sentence, and let it go. Somebody else, somewhere, opens the app and finds it. They will never know who wrote it. You will never know who read it. But for a moment, two strangers will be connected by something good.

There are no profiles, no follower counts, and nothing to scroll. Just one letter at a time, when you decide to open the door.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Write a letter. Put a thought into a single sentence and let it drift. Someone will find it.

Receive a letter. Open the app and a small light from a stranger lands in your hand.

Save what stays with you. The Vault keeps your favourites in a private place that is only yours.

Pair with someone you love. Share a private code with a friend or partner, and the two of you can send messages just between you.

Pick a mood. Playful, peaceful, motivating, or romantic. Or leave it to the wind.

Soft Mode for sensitive days. When you need gentler words, Lumi remembers.

WHO LUMI IS FOR

For anyone who has ever wished a stranger would say something kind today. For nights that feel a little long. For mornings that need a small push. For hearts who want to remind someone they are loved.

A QUIET DESIGN

Lumi was built for stillness. Soft pinks, warm cream, serif typography. No notifications shouting for attention. No feed to scroll. Just one sentence, when you choose to open the door.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No accounts. No public profile. Pair codes are anonymous. Notifications, if you allow them, only arrive when something genuinely warm is ready to be shared. Unkind words are removed quietly before reaching anyone.

ON YOUR WRIST

The new Apple Watch app keeps a deck of gentle letters one Crown turn away. The lock screen and watch face complications carry a single sentence with you through the day.

Lumi is a quiet corner of the internet. May it find you on a day you needed it.
